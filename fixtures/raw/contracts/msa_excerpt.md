# Master Services Agreement (Excerpt)

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form within
thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Fees are stated in United States dollars
and are exclusive of taxes.

4.2 Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the lesser of
one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by
applicable law.

4.3 Notwithstanding Section 4.2, no interest shall accrue on amounts that
Customer disputes in good faith and in writing within fifteen (15) days of the
invoice date, provided Customer pays all undisputed amounts when due.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial
Term specified in the Order Form.

5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon thirty
(30) days written notice, provided the breaching party has not cured the breach
within that period.

5.3 Upon termination, Customer shall pay all amounts accrued through the
effective date of termination, and the provisions of Sections 4, 7, and 9 shall
survive.
