# Legal Proceedings

From time to time, we are involved in legal proceedings and claims arising
in the ordinary course of business, including proceedings related to
intellectual property, employment matters, and commercial disputes.

## Material pending matters

In March 2023, a former supplier filed a complaint alleging breach of a
component purchase agreement and seeking approximately $4.2 million in
damages. We believe the claims are without merit and intend to defend the
matter vigorously. Based on information currently available, we do not
believe the outcome of this matter will have a material adverse effect on
our financial condition.

In September 2023, we received a demand letter from a competitor alleging
infringement of a patent related to autonomous navigation. We have not been
served with a formal complaint, and we do not currently believe this matter
is material, though litigation outcomes are inherently uncertain.

## General litigation risk

We are also subject to routine litigation and claims incidental to our
business, none of which we currently believe, individually or in the
aggregate, would have a material adverse effect on our business, financial
condition, or results of operations. Regardless of outcome, litigation can
have an adverse impact on us because of defense costs, diversion of
management resources, and other factors.

## Accruals

We record an accrual for a loss contingency when a loss is probable and the
amount can be reasonably estimated. As of fiscal year end, accrued
contingent liabilities related to legal matters were not material to our
consolidated financial statements.
