# Risk Factors

Investing in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. You should
carefully consider the risks described below, together with the other
information in this filing, before making an investment decision.

## Risks related to our business

We depend on a small number of large customers for a significant portion of
revenue. Our five largest customers accounted for approximately 61% of
revenue in fiscal 2023. The loss of, or a significant reduction in orders
from, any of these customers would materially harm our results of
operations.

Our sales cycle with large enterprise customers is long and unpredictable,
often spanning twelve to eighteen months from initial engagement to a signed
purchase order, which makes near-term revenue difficult to forecast
precisely.

## Risks related to supply chain

We rely on a limited number of suppliers for key components, including
lidar sensors and motor controllers. A prolonged disruption at any single
supplier could delay shipments and damage customer relationships. We do not
currently maintain long-term supply agreements with our two largest lidar
suppliers.

## Risks related to competition

The market for warehouse automation robotics is intensely competitive and
includes both well-capitalized incumbents and well-funded private
companies. Some competitors have greater financial, technical, and
marketing resources than we do, which could allow them to respond more
quickly to new technologies or pricing pressure.

## Risks related to intellectual property

Our success depends in part on our ability to protect our proprietary
technology. Third parties may claim that our products infringe their
intellectual property rights, and defending against such claims could be
costly and time-consuming regardless of the outcome.

## Risks related to our workforce

Our future success depends on our ability to attract and retain highly
skilled engineering personnel in a competitive labor market for robotics
and machine learning talent, particularly in the San Francisco Bay Area
where our engineering headquarters is located.
