## Seasonality

Our business exhibits seasonal patterns tied to our customers' own
fulfillment peaks. Fourth-quarter revenue is typically the strongest as
customers complete deployments ahead of the holiday shopping season, and
first-quarter revenue is typically the softest as customer capital budgets
reset.

## Sequential trends

Revenue grew sequentially in each of the last six quarters presented, and
net income has been positive in every quarter since Q2 of fiscal 2022,
reflecting the operating leverage gained as Software & Services revenue has
scaled against a largely fixed engineering cost base.
