## Notes on comparability

Fiscal 2021 results reflect the acquisition of Fetchline Systems in the
fourth quarter of that year, which contributed approximately $18.6 million
of incremental revenue and $6.2 million of acquisition-related operating
expenses. Excluding these items, fiscal 2021 operating loss would have been
approximately $1.2 million narrower.

## Per-share data basis

Diluted earnings per share is computed using the treasury stock method for
outstanding stock options and unvested restricted stock units. Weighted
average diluted shares outstanding were 54.7 million, 53.9 million, and 51.2
million for fiscal 2023, 2022, and 2021 respectively.
