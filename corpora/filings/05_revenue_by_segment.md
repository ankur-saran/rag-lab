# Revenue by Segment

We report results in two operating segments: Hardware and Software &
Services. Hardware consists of robot unit sales; Software & Services
consists of our fleet-management subscription and post-sale support
contracts.

## Segment revenue

| Segment ($M) | FY2023 | FY2022 | FY2021 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware | 312.9 | 267.0 | 213.4 |
| Software & Services | 99.5 | 71.0 | 47.7 |
| Total revenue | 412.4 | 338.0 | 261.1 |

## Segment gross margin

| Segment | FY2023 | FY2022 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware | 38.4% | 34.9% |
| Software & Services | 71.2% | 66.8% |

## Revenue by geography

| Region ($M) | FY2023 | FY2022 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 301.5 | 253.6 |
| Europe | 74.8 | 57.1 |
| Rest of world | 36.1 | 27.3 |

## Discussion

Software & Services revenue grew faster than Hardware for the third
consecutive year, reflecting continued attachment of fleet-management
subscriptions to both new and previously-deployed robot fleets. We expect
Software & Services to represent an increasing share of total revenue over
time, consistent with our stated strategy of building a recurring revenue
base on top of the installed hardware fleet.
