# Management's Discussion and Analysis — Overview

The following discussion should be read together with the consolidated
financial statements and related notes included elsewhere in this filing.
Meridian Robotics, Inc. ("Meridian," "we," "us," or "the Company") designs
and manufactures autonomous mobile robots for warehouse fulfillment.

## Fiscal 2023 highlights

- Total revenue grew 22% year over year to $412.4 million
- Gross margin expanded to 46.1% from 41.8% in fiscal 2022
- We shipped 3,140 robot units, up from 2,205 in the prior year
- Cash and cash equivalents at year end were $187.2 million

## Key operating metrics

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2022 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue ($M) | 412.4 | 338.0 | +22.0% |
| Gross margin | 46.1% | 41.8% | +4.3 pts |
| Units shipped | 3,140 | 2,205 | +42.4% |
| Operating expenses ($M) | 148.9 | 129.6 | +14.9% |
| Net income ($M) | 31.7 | 12.4 | +155.6% |

## Drivers of revenue growth

Revenue growth was primarily driven by expanded deployments with existing
customers in the grocery fulfillment vertical, and by the initial commercial
shipments of our Gen4 platform, which began in the third quarter of fiscal
2023. Average selling price per unit increased 8% year over year, reflecting
a richer mix of Gen4 units relative to the legacy Gen3 platform.

## Outlook

We expect fiscal 2024 revenue growth to moderate to the mid-teens percentage
range as the initial wave of Gen4 upgrade orders from existing customers
normalizes, partially offset by new customer wins expected to close in the
second half of the fiscal year.
