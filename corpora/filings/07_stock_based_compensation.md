## RSU activity

Unvested restricted stock units outstanding at fiscal year end totaled 3.1
million shares, with a weighted average grant-date fair value of $34.60 per
share. During fiscal 2023 we granted 1.4 million RSUs, 0.9 million vested,
and 0.2 million were forfeited.

## Valuation assumptions for options

Stock options are valued using the Black-Scholes model. Key assumptions
used for options granted in fiscal 2023 included an expected term of 6.1
years, expected volatility of 42%, a risk-free rate of 4.2%, and no
expected dividend yield, consistent with our history of not paying cash
dividends.

## Unrecognized compensation cost

As of fiscal year end, total unrecognized stock-based compensation expense
related to unvested awards was $71.4 million, expected to be recognized
over a weighted average period of 2.7 years.
