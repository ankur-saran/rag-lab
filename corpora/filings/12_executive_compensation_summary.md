## Compensation philosophy

Our executive compensation program is designed to attract and retain
experienced leadership and align executive incentives with long-term
stockholder value creation. A substantial majority of target compensation
for named executive officers is delivered in the form of equity awards
that vest over four years, subject to continued service.

## Annual bonus plan

Annual cash bonuses are tied to Company-wide financial targets — revenue and
non-GAAP operating margin — weighted 60% and 40% respectively, with a
individual-performance modifier applied by the compensation committee of up
to plus or minus 20% of the formulaic result.

## CEO pay ratio

The ratio of our CEO's total fiscal 2023 compensation to that of our median
employee was approximately 58 to 1. This ratio was calculated using total
compensation as determined under applicable SEC rules and reflects our
global employee population, including manufacturing and assembly staff at
the Reno facility.
