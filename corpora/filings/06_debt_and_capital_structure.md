## Term loan covenants

The term loan agreement requires us to maintain a minimum liquidity balance
of $20.0 million and a maximum total net leverage ratio of 3.5x trailing
twelve-month EBITDA, tested quarterly. We were in compliance with all
covenants as of fiscal year end.

## Equity structure

We have two classes of common stock. Class A common stock is entitled to
one vote per share and Class B common stock is entitled to ten votes per
share. Class B shares are held primarily by our founders and convert
automatically to Class A shares upon transfer to a non-permitted holder.

## Share repurchase program

Our board authorized a $25.0 million share repurchase program in the third
quarter of fiscal 2023. As of fiscal year end, we had repurchased 412,000
shares of Class A common stock for approximately $6.8 million under this
program, leaving $18.2 million of remaining authorization.

## Debt maturities

Scheduled principal payments on the term loan and equipment financing notes
over the next five fiscal years total $9.2 million in FY2024, $9.8 million
in FY2025, $10.1 million in FY2026, $10.5 million in FY2027, and $7.8
million thereafter.
