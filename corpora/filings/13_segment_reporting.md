# Segment Reporting

We operate in two reportable segments, Hardware and Software & Services,
consistent with how our Chief Operating Decision Maker reviews performance
and allocates resources.

## Segment operating income

| Segment ($M) | FY2023 | FY2022 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware operating income | 22.6 | 4.1 |
| Software & Services operating income | 38.9 | 24.7 |
| Unallocated corporate expenses | (20.3) | (17.2) |
| Total operating income | 41.2 | 11.6 |

## Segment assets

| Segment ($M) | FY2023 | FY2022 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware | 398.1 | 331.4 |
| Software & Services | 89.7 | 62.0 |
| Corporate and unallocated | 125.0 | 104.9 |

## Basis of segmentation

Unallocated corporate expenses consist primarily of general corporate
functions, including finance, legal, and executive management, that are not
directly attributable to either segment. Segment assets exclude
intercompany balances that eliminate in consolidation.

## Intersegment transactions

Software & Services occasionally bundles a discounted Hardware unit as part
of a combined contract. Revenue allocated to each segment in these bundled
arrangements is based on relative standalone selling price, consistent with
our revenue recognition policy described in the notes to the financial
statements.
