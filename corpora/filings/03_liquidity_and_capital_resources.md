# Liquidity and Capital Resources

## Sources of liquidity

As of the end of fiscal 2023, we had $187.2 million in cash and cash
equivalents and $50.0 million of undrawn capacity under our revolving credit
facility. We believe our existing cash, together with cash generated from
operations and available borrowing capacity, will be sufficient to meet our
working capital and capital expenditure needs for at least the next twelve
months.

## Cash flow summary

| Cash flow category ($M) | FY2023 | FY2022 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating activities | 58.3 | 22.1 |
| Investing activities | (41.7) | (35.2) |
| Financing activities | (6.9) | 48.6 |
| Net change in cash | 9.7 | 35.5 |

## Operating cash flow

The increase in cash provided by operating activities was primarily
attributable to higher net income and improved working capital management,
particularly a reduction in days sales outstanding from 58 days to 49 days
as we tightened payment terms with several large fulfillment-center
customers.

## Investing activities

Cash used in investing activities consisted primarily of $34.8 million in
capital expenditures related to expanding our robotics testing and assembly
facility in Reno, Nevada, and $6.9 million in capitalized software
development costs.

## Financing activities

Financing activities in fiscal 2023 consisted primarily of $9.1 million in
proceeds from stock option exercises, offset by $16.0 million in scheduled
term loan repayments. Fiscal 2022 financing activities reflected $55.0
million in gross proceeds from the term loan facility entered into that
year.
