# Properties

Our corporate headquarters and primary engineering offices are located in
the San Francisco Bay Area, California, under a lease expiring in 2028. We
also lease an assembly and testing facility in Reno, Nevada, and smaller
sales offices in Amsterdam and Austin.

## Facility summary

| Location | Function | Square feet | Lease expiration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| San Francisco, CA | Headquarters / engineering | 118,000 | 2028 |
| Reno, NV | Assembly and testing | 240,000 | 2031 |
| Amsterdam, NL | European sales office | 12,000 | 2026 |
| Austin, TX | Sales and support | 9,500 | 2025 |

## Facility utilization

We believe our existing facilities are adequate to meet our current needs
and that suitable additional space will be available on commercially
reasonable terms as needed to accommodate future growth. The Reno facility
is currently operating at approximately 65% of its designed assembly
capacity, providing runway for unit volume growth without near-term
expansion.

## Manufacturing approach

We do not own manufacturing facilities for component fabrication. Our Reno
facility performs final assembly, calibration, and quality testing using
components sourced from contract manufacturers, consistent with our
asset-light approach to hardware production.
