## Purchase commitments detail

Purchase commitments consist primarily of non-cancelable component orders
placed with our lidar and motor controller suppliers to secure manufacturing
capacity for the following two fiscal quarters. These commitments are
typically placed 6-9 months ahead of expected component delivery given
current industry lead times.

## Operating leases

Operating lease obligations relate primarily to our Reno, Nevada assembly
facility (lease expires 2031) and our San Francisco Bay Area engineering
headquarters (lease expires 2028). Neither lease contains a purchase option.

## Off-balance-sheet arrangements

We do not have any off-balance-sheet arrangements, as defined by applicable
SEC rules, that have or are reasonably likely to have a material effect on
our financial condition.
