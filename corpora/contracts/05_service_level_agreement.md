# Service Level Agreement

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") sets forth the availability and
support commitments applicable to the Service and Customer's exclusive
remedies for Provider's failure to meet them.

## 1. Availability Commitment

1.1 Provider commits to at least 99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage, measured
as the total number of minutes in a calendar month minus Downtime minutes,
divided by the total number of minutes in that month.

1.2 "Downtime" means any period during which the Service returns error
responses to more than 5% of requests for more than five (5) consecutive
minutes, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Excused Downtime.

## 2. Exclusions

2.1 Downtime does not include unavailability caused by: (a) Scheduled
Maintenance performed with at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice; (b)
factors outside Provider's reasonable control, including denial-of-service
attacks or internet backbone failures; (c) Customer's equipment or
third-party services not provided by Provider; or (d) suspension of the
Service in accordance with the underlying agreement.

## 3. Service Credits

3.1 If Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the commitment in Section 1.1,
Customer is eligible for a service credit according to the following
schedule: 99.0% to 99.9% — 10% of monthly fees; 95.0% to 99.0% — 25% of
monthly fees; below 95.0% — 50% of monthly fees.

3.2 To receive a service credit, Customer must submit a request within
thirty (30) days of the end of the affected month. Service credits are
Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the
availability commitment.

## 4. Support Response Times

4.1 Provider shall respond to support tickets according to severity:
Critical — one (1) hour; High — four (4) business hours; Normal — one (1)
business day; Low — three (3) business days.

4.2 "Critical" means the Service is entirely unavailable to all Customer
Authorized Users. Provider, acting reasonably, determines severity
classification unless the applicable Order Form specifies otherwise.

## 5. Measurement and Reporting

5.1 Provider measures availability using its internal monitoring systems.
Customer may request the underlying availability data for any month within
twelve (12) months of that month, and Provider shall provide it within ten
(10) business days of such request.
