# Amendment and Waiver

This Amendment ("Amendment") modifies the Master Services Agreement dated
as of the original Effective Date (the "Original Agreement") between the
parties. Capitalized terms not defined in this Amendment have the meanings
given in the Original Agreement.

## 1. Background

1.1 The parties entered into the Original Agreement to govern the
provision of Services. The parties now wish to amend certain provisions of
the Original Agreement as set forth below.

## 2. Amendments

2.1 Section 3.1 of the Original Agreement (Fees and Payment) is deleted in
its entirety and replaced with: "Customer shall pay all fees set forth in
the applicable Order Form within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date."

2.2 Section 6.1 of the Original Agreement (Limitation of Liability) is
amended by replacing "twelve (12) months" with "eighteen (18) months" in
both places it appears.

2.3 A new Section 7 is added to the Original Agreement, titled "Data
Residency," providing that Provider shall store Customer Data exclusively
within the United States unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Waiver

3.1 Customer waives, solely with respect to the invoice issued for the
period immediately preceding this Amendment, any claim for late-payment
interest that accrued under the original forty-five-day payment term prior
to this Amendment's effective date, provided payment of the underlying
invoice is made within ten (10) business days of execution of this
Amendment.

3.2 The waiver in Section 3.1 is limited to the specific invoice and time
period described and does not constitute a waiver of any other right or a
waiver of future compliance with any term of the Original Agreement as
amended.

## 4. No Other Changes

4.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms and
conditions of the Original Agreement remain in full force and effect. In
the event of a conflict between this Amendment and the Original Agreement,
this Amendment controls.

## 5. Effective Date

5.1 This Amendment is effective as of the date of the last signature below
and applies prospectively unless expressly stated otherwise in Section 3.
