# Employment Agreement (Excerpt)

This excerpt covers the compensation, confidentiality, and post-employment
provisions of the Employment Agreement between the Company and Employee.

## 1. Position and Duties

1.1 Employee shall serve in the position set forth in the offer letter and
shall devote substantially all of Employee's business time and attention
to the Company's business, except as otherwise approved in writing.

## 2. Compensation

2.1 The Company shall pay Employee the base salary set forth in the offer
letter, payable in accordance with the Company's standard payroll
practices, subject to applicable withholding.

2.2 Employee is eligible for an annual discretionary bonus, subject to
Company and individual performance, as determined by the Company in its
sole discretion. Nothing in this Section 2.2 guarantees any bonus amount.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 Employee acknowledges that in the course of employment, Employee will
have access to Confidential Information of the Company and agrees not to
use or disclose such information except as necessary to perform Employee's
duties, both during and after employment.

## 4. Assignment of Inventions

4.1 Employee agrees that all inventions, discoveries, and works of
authorship conceived or created during employment and related to the
Company's business are the sole property of the Company, and Employee
hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such inventions to the
Company.

## 5. At-Will Employment

5.1 Employee's employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party
at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, subject to
any separate written agreement providing otherwise.

## 6. Post-Employment Restrictions

6.1 For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of
employment, Employee shall not solicit any employee of the Company to
leave the Company's employment.

6.2 The restrictions in Section 6.1 apply regardless of the reason for
termination and are subject to any limitations imposed by applicable law
in the jurisdiction governing this Agreement.
