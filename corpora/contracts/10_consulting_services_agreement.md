# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs the engagement of
Consultant by Client to perform the services described in a Statement of
Work ("SOW").

## 1. Services and Statements of Work

1.1 Consultant shall perform the services described in each SOW in
accordance with the schedule, deliverables, and fees set forth therein.
Each SOW is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

1.2 Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee of Client.
Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or agency
relationship between the parties.

## 2. Fees and Expenses

2.1 Client shall pay Consultant's fees as set forth in the applicable SOW,
either on a fixed-fee or time-and-materials basis as specified.

2.2 Client shall reimburse Consultant for reasonable, pre-approved
out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services, provided
Consultant submits receipts within thirty (30) days of incurring the
expense.

## 3. Ownership of Work Product

3.1 Except for Consultant's pre-existing tools and methodologies, all
deliverables created specifically for Client under a SOW are "work made for
hire" and, to the extent not legally deemed such, are hereby assigned to
Client upon full payment for the applicable SOW.

3.2 Consultant retains ownership of any pre-existing intellectual property,
tools, or methodologies used in performing the Services, and grants Client
a non-exclusive license to use such items solely as incorporated into the
deliverables.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using at
least reasonable care and shall use it solely to perform or receive the
Services.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 This Agreement continues until all SOWs are completed or terminated.
Either party may terminate a SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days
written notice, in which case Client shall pay for Services performed
through the termination date.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

6.1 Consultant's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed
the fees paid under the SOW giving rise to the claim in the twelve (12)
months preceding the claim.
