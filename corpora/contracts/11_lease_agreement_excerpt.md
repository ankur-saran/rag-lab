# Commercial Lease Agreement (Excerpt)

This excerpt covers the rent, maintenance, and default provisions of the
Lease Agreement between Landlord and Tenant for the Premises described in
Exhibit A.

## 1. Term

1.1 The lease term commences on the Commencement Date and continues for
the period specified in the Basic Lease Information, unless extended
pursuant to any renewal option or terminated earlier as provided herein.

## 2. Rent

2.1 Tenant shall pay Base Rent in the amount set forth in the Basic Lease
Information, payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month.

2.2 Base Rent increases by three percent (3%) annually on each anniversary
of the Commencement Date.

2.3 In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Proportionate Share
of Operating Expenses, estimated monthly and reconciled annually as
described in Section 4.

## 3. Use of Premises

3.1 Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office and
engineering purposes and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior
written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 4. Operating Expenses

4.1 "Operating Expenses" means all reasonable costs of operating,
maintaining, and repairing the building and common areas, including
property taxes, insurance, utilities for common areas, and management
fees, but excluding capital improvements except as amortized in accordance
with generally accepted accounting principles.

4.2 Landlord shall provide Tenant an annual reconciliation statement within
ninety (90) days after each calendar year. Any underpayment is due within
thirty (30) days of the statement; any overpayment is credited against
future rent.

## 5. Maintenance and Repairs

5.1 Landlord shall maintain the structural elements, roof, and common
areas. Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises and promptly
notify Landlord of any condition requiring Landlord's repair.

## 6. Default

6.1 Tenant's failure to pay rent within five (5) days of written notice
that payment is past due constitutes an event of default, entitling
Landlord to the remedies available under applicable law, including
termination of the lease and recovery of unpaid rent.
