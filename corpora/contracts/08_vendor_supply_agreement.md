# Vendor Supply Agreement

This Vendor Supply Agreement ("Agreement") governs Buyer's purchase of
components from Supplier pursuant to Purchase Orders issued under this
Agreement.

## 1. Orders

1.1 Buyer shall submit Purchase Orders specifying quantity, delivery date,
and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject a Purchase Order
within five (5) business days; a Purchase Order not rejected within that
period is deemed accepted.

1.2 The terms of this Agreement govern over any conflicting terms in a
Purchase Order or acknowledgment, unless the parties expressly agree
otherwise in writing for a specific Purchase Order.

## 2. Delivery and Risk of Loss

2.1 Supplier shall deliver components in accordance with the delivery date
on the accepted Purchase Order. Time is of the essence for delivery dates
for components used in Buyer's production schedule.

2.2 Risk of loss transfers to Buyer upon delivery to the carrier at
Supplier's shipping dock, unless the Purchase Order specifies otherwise.

## 3. Quality and Inspection

3.1 Supplier warrants that all components conform to the specifications
set forth in the applicable Purchase Order and are free from defects in
materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months from delivery.

3.2 Buyer may inspect components upon receipt and reject any that do not
conform to specifications by notifying Supplier within thirty (30) days of
delivery. Non-conforming components shall be replaced or credited at
Buyer's election.

## 4. Pricing and Payment

4.1 Prices are as set forth in Exhibit A and remain fixed for the Initial
Term. Supplier may propose price changes for subsequent terms upon ninety
(90) days notice.

4.2 Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of
receipt.

## 5. Forecasts and Capacity

5.1 Buyer shall provide Supplier a rolling twelve-month non-binding
forecast, updated monthly. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable
efforts to maintain manufacturing capacity sufficient to meet forecasted
volumes, but the forecast does not constitute a purchase commitment.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 This Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years. Either party may
terminate for uncured material breach upon thirty (30) days written
notice, or for the other party's insolvency upon written notice.
