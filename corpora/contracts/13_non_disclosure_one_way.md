# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement

This One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
Disclosing Party and Receiving Party in connection with Disclosing Party's
provision of Confidential Information for the Purpose described below.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Disclosing Party may share Confidential Information with Receiving
Party solely for Receiving Party to evaluate a potential engagement, and
for no other purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public technical, business,
and financial information disclosed by Disclosing Party, whether marked
confidential or not, that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of
disclosure.

2.2 Confidential Information excludes information that is or becomes
publicly available through no fault of Receiving Party, was already known
to Receiving Party without restriction, or is independently developed
without reference to Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party

3.1 Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose
other than the Purpose, and shall not disclose it to any third party
except employees and advisors who need to know it for the Purpose and are
bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this
Agreement.

3.2 Receiving Party shall promptly notify Disclosing Party of any
unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information that comes to
Receiving Party's attention.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement remains in effect for eighteen (18) months from the
Effective Date. The confidentiality obligations survive for two (2) years
following the date of each disclosure.

## 5. No Warranty

5.1 Confidential Information is provided "as is." Disclosing Party makes
no representation or warranty regarding the accuracy or completeness of
any Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement.

## 6. Remedies

6.1 Receiving Party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure of
Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary
damages would be an inadequate remedy, and Disclosing Party is entitled to
seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedies.
