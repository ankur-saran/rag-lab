# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements and forms part of the
underlying agreement between the parties (the "Principal Agreement") and
applies to the extent Provider processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data," "Processing," "Controller," and "Processor" have the
meanings given in applicable Data Protection Law.

1.2 "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Provider to process
Personal Data in connection with the Services.

## 2. Roles of the Parties

2.1 As between the parties, Customer is the Controller and Provider is the
Processor with respect to Personal Data processed under this DPA.

2.2 Provider shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions
from Customer, including with regard to transfers, unless required to do
otherwise by applicable law.

## 3. Sub-processors

3.1 Customer authorizes Provider to engage the Sub-processors listed in
Exhibit A as of the Effective Date.

3.2 Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days prior notice of
any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor, during which
Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. If the parties
cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected Services.

3.3 Provider remains liable for the acts and omissions of its
Sub-processors to the same extent Provider would be liable if performing
the services directly.

## 4. Security Measures

4.1 Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and
organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or
unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage,
as described in Exhibit B.

4.2 Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event
within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data
breach affecting Customer's Personal Data.

## 5. Data Subject Requests

5.1 Provider shall, taking into account the nature of the processing,
assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures in
fulfilling Customer's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects
exercising their rights under applicable Data Protection Law.

## 6. Deletion and Return of Data

6.1 Upon termination of the Services, Provider shall, at Customer's
election, delete or return all Personal Data, and delete existing copies,
within sixty (60) days, unless applicable law requires continued storage.
