# Reseller Agreement

This Reseller Agreement ("Agreement") authorizes Reseller to market and
resell Provider's products to end customers in the Territory, subject to
the terms below.

## 1. Appointment

1.1 Provider appoints Reseller as a non-exclusive reseller of the Products
in the Territory described in Exhibit A during the Term.

1.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts Provider from appointing other
resellers or selling directly to end customers in the Territory.

## 2. Pricing and Discounts

2.1 Reseller shall purchase Products at the discount off list price set
forth in Exhibit B, which varies based on Reseller's tier and the volume
commitments in Section 3.

2.2 Provider may change list prices upon sixty (60) days written notice.
Discount percentages remain fixed for the remainder of the then-current
Term regardless of list price changes.

## 3. Volume Commitments and Tiers

3.1 Reseller's tier is determined by trailing twelve-month purchase volume:
Silver — under $250,000; Gold — $250,000 to $999,999; Platinum — $1,000,000
and above.

3.2 Tier is recalculated quarterly. A Reseller that falls below its current
tier's threshold for two consecutive quarters is moved to the appropriate
lower tier effective the following quarter.

## 4. End Customer Terms

4.1 Reseller shall ensure that each end customer agreement incorporates
terms no less protective of Provider than the standard end-user terms
provided by Provider, including limitation of liability and intellectual
property provisions.

4.2 Reseller shall not make any representation or warranty regarding the
Products beyond those Provider makes available in writing.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year and renews annually
unless either party gives ninety (90) days notice of non-renewal.

5.2 Provider may terminate immediately upon Reseller's insolvency, breach
of Section 4, or unauthorized use of Provider's trademarks.

## 6. Trademark License

6.1 Provider grants Reseller a limited, non-exclusive license to use
Provider's trademarks solely to market the Products during the Term, in
accordance with Provider's brand guidelines as updated from time to time.
