# Strategic Partnership Agreement (Excerpt)

This excerpt covers the collaboration, revenue-sharing, and exclusivity
provisions of the Strategic Partnership Agreement between Company A and
Company B.

## 1. Scope of Collaboration

1.1 The parties will collaborate to develop an integration between
Company A's platform and Company B's platform (the "Integration") as
described in the joint technical specification attached as Exhibit A.

1.2 Each party is responsible for the development, hosting, and support of
its own platform's side of the Integration, coordinated through the joint
steering committee described in Section 5.

## 2. Revenue Sharing

2.1 For any joint customer that purchases both parties' products as part
of a bundled offering, the parties shall share revenue according to the
allocation methodology set forth in Exhibit B, reconciled quarterly.

2.2 Neither party is obligated to bundle its product with the other's, and
either party may continue to sell its product independently.

## 3. Exclusivity

3.1 During the Term, neither party shall enter into a substantially
similar integration partnership with a direct competitor of the other
party, as identified in Exhibit C, without the other party's prior written
consent.

3.2 The exclusivity in Section 3.1 does not restrict either party's
existing relationships in effect prior to the Effective Date, which are
listed in Exhibit C for transparency.

## 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual
property. Any jointly developed intellectual property arising specifically
from the Integration work is jointly owned, with each party free to
exploit it without accounting to the other, subject to the confidentiality
obligations herein.

## 5. Governance

5.1 The parties shall establish a joint steering committee, meeting
quarterly, to review integration roadmap, resolve technical
disagreements, and monitor the revenue-sharing reconciliation.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 This Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years and renews
automatically for one-year terms unless either party gives ninety (90)
days notice of non-renewal.
