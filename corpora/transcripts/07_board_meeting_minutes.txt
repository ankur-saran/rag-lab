Board of Directors Meeting Minutes — Solstice Analytics, Inc.
Present: Priya Chandrasekaran (CEO), Marcus Webb (CFO), four independent directors, general counsel (recording)

The meeting was called to order. The chair noted a quorum was present. Minutes from the prior meeting were approved without objection.

Marcus Webb presented the quarterly financial review. Revenue for the quarter was 96.8 million dollars, in line with the board-approved plan. Marcus walked through the deceleration in year-over-year growth rate, attributing it primarily to a difficult prior-year comparison rather than any change in competitive positioning, and directors asked several clarifying questions about the smallest-customer-tier churn dynamic discussed in the prior quarter, which management confirmed has stabilized.

Priya Chandrasekaran presented the Ferrostat acquisition for formal ratification. The board had previously reviewed and approved the transaction terms via written consent prior to closing; this presentation served as a full debrief. Priya covered the strategic rationale, integration plan, and key personnel retention terms for the Ferrostat founding team, who are subject to a two-year retention structure. One director asked about cultural integration risk given the target company's smaller size; Priya noted the product organization has run two prior smaller acqui-hires and has a documented integration playbook being applied here.

The board then moved to a discussion of the fiscal 2025 operating plan. Marcus presented preliminary revenue guidance of 445 to 455 million dollars and walked through the underlying assumptions, including planned headcount growth of approximately 22 percent, concentrated in engineering and go-to-market for the new Asia-Pacific office. Directors discussed the pace of the Singapore office build-out and asked management to return with a more detailed twelve-month hiring and revenue plan for that region at the next meeting.

The compensation committee chair provided an update on the annual executive compensation review, noting the committee's intention to bring formal recommendations to the full board next quarter following completion of the peer benchmarking study currently in progress.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned. The next regular meeting is scheduled for the following quarter, with an interim call available if needed to address the Asia-Pacific plan ahead of that date.
